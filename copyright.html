Copyright Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

MusicBusiness Arena respects intellectual property rights and expects all users to do the same.

Artist Responsibility

Artists must only upload content they own or have legal permission to distribute.

Copyright Complaints

If you believe copyrighted content has been uploaded without authorization, please submit a copyright complaint containing:

  • Your name and contact information
  • Description of the copyrighted work
  • URL of the allegedly infringing content
  • Proof of ownership

Content Removal

Upon receiving a valid complaint, MusicBusiness Arena may investigate and remove content where appropriate.

Repeat Infringers

Accounts repeatedly violating copyright laws may be suspended or permanently terminated.
