DMCA Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

MusicBusiness Arena respects the intellectual property rights of artists, creators, copyright owners, and other rights holders.

Users may only upload content they own or have the legal right to distribute. This includes music, artwork, videos, photographs, lyrics, and other content uploaded to the platform.

Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe content available on MusicBusiness Arena infringes your copyright, you may submit a copyright complaint requesting removal of the content.

Required Information

A copyright complaint should include:

  • Your full name and contact information
  • Description of the copyrighted work
  • URL or location of the allegedly infringing content
  • Proof of ownership or authorization
  • A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is unauthorized
  • A statement that the information provided is accurate

Content Removal

Upon receiving a valid complaint, MusicBusiness Arena may remove or disable access to the reported content while investigating the claim.

Counter Notification

Users who believe their content was removed in error may submit a counter notification with supporting information demonstrating their rights to the content.

Repeat Infringers

Accounts repeatedly found to violate copyright laws may be suspended, restricted, or permanently terminated.

Artist Responsibility

Artists are solely responsible for ensuring they possess all rights, licenses, permissions, and ownership required to upload and distribute content on MusicBusiness Arena.

Contact

Copyright complaints and DMCA notices should be submitted through the Contact page.
